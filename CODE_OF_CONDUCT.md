# Community conduct

Treat participants respectfully. Critique evidence and technical work rather than
individuals. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disclosure
of private data are not acceptable. Credit original authors and distinguish factual
claims from interpretation. Make room for good-faith questions and corrections.

The owner must appoint maintainers and a private reporting route before public
community launch. This file does not invent an enforcement team or contact address.
